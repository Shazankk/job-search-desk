Job Search Desk
How it works Get started GitHub

Find jobs that fit, and see exactly why.

Job Search Desk runs on your Mac. It searches free job sources for roles that match your resume, scores each one with a plain explanation, and prepares a tailored resume using only what you have already written.

Download for Mac See the code on GitHub

Free and open source. Needs macOS and Python 3.11 or newer. Setup takes about five minutes.

Your resume stays yours.

For each job worth applying to, it writes a Word resume with your strongest, most relevant points first. It never adds a skill, a title or a number you did not write.

Next to it you get a short brief on the role, ideas for who to contact, and a draft message. A provenance file records every paragraph, so you can check nothing was changed.

For any career, anywhere.

Type the places you want to work however you like. It understands about 630 cities in 97 countries, and asks you for the country when it meets a town it does not know.

It reads your field from your resume, from nursing and teaching to software and sales. Careers outside its list still work from the skills on your resume.

Where it looks for jobs

The free sources need no account. It hides reposts and copies of the same job, and shows you which skills your matches keep asking for.

Remote jobs

Himalayas, filtered to roles open to people in your country.

Adzuna

Optional and off until you turn it on. It is unofficial and can stop working.

Get started on your Mac

  1. Install Python

    Download the latest Python 3 for macOS from python.org and run the installer. Skip this if you already have Python 3.11 or newer.

  2. Download and unzip

    Use the download button, open the ZIP, and move the job-search-desk-main folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents.

  3. Run setup

    In the folder, right-click SETUP.command and choose Open. macOS asks once because the file came from the internet. Setup installs what it needs and opens the dashboard in your browser.

  4. Add your resume and search

    In Resume & settings, upload your resume and set your places. Then click Find & prepare jobs. Next time, just double-click OPEN_DASHBOARD.command.

What it will not do

  • Apply for jobs or message anyone. You review everything and apply yourself.
  • Invent experience, skills or achievements.
  • Promise interviews. Scores are a guide built from keywords, not hiring odds.
  • Find every job. No single source lists them all, so keep your usual job alerts too.

Your data stays on your Mac

  • Your resume, job history and documents are saved in the folder you choose.
  • Searches send only the role and place, never your resume.
  • Passwords and keys go in your Mac's Keychain.
  • AI is optional and off unless you add your own key and agree to share job text.
